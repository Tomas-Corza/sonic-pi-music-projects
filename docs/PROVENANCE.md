# Project Notes

## Intense Deep / Tech House — July 2026
A 126 BPM house project exploring drum programming, bass, acid-style leads, melodic arpeggios, and adjustable mix levels.

## Advanced Shoegaze House — July 2026
A 120 BPM experiment combining shoegaze-inspired harmony with house rhythms, distorted chord textures, and atmospheric synthesis.

## Dark Futuristic House — August 2026
A 124 BPM composition in G♯ minor, built around the G♯m–E–B–F♯ progression with layered bass, pads, melodies, and glitch accents.

## Techno Groove — August 2026
A 140 BPM techno project featuring driving drums, synth stabs, bass, and a 16th-note sine-wave swell.

## Playback verification

All four scripts were tested in Sonic Pi and confirmed working by Tomas Corza on November 7, 2026.
